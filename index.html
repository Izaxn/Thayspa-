Good to know

Booking policies

Booking & arrival

Please arrive 10 minutes early for a brief check-in, especially for your first visit. First-time guests get a short consultation so we can tailor the session to you.

Cancellations & rescheduling

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment. Late cancellations and no-shows may be subject to a fee.

Health & hygiene

Linens and tools are sanitized between every session. Please let us know about allergies, pregnancy, or any medical conditions before your treatment begins.

Payment & packages

Card and cash are both accepted. Packages are non-refundable but transferable to another person, and expire 6 months from purchase.
